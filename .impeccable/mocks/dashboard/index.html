Trends dashboard: three directions

Each mockup uses the same synthetic 14 days of checks and covers the same chart views: since dose, across days, by test, left vs right and frequency, plus each direction's own signature view. Every view has a chart/table toggle, and a Plain/Detail switch serves patients and neurologists from one screen.

  1. A. Timetable

    Rolled direction · top-bar shell

    Swiss-railway clarity: a blue shell, tabular columns, and red kept only for doses. Its signature is the Day timetable, with 14 rows of doses as departures, checks as graded dots, and the running time of each dose interval.

    Open A

  2. B. Pill organizer

    Impeccable’s pick · side-rail shell · familiar by design

    The weekly organizer on every patient's table. Its signature is a 14-day × 3-dose compartment grid, each compartment tinted by the tremor measured in that interval. Click a compartment to open its detail.

    Open B

  3. C. Star atlas

    Challenger · plate-led shell · desk (light) and field (dark) editions

    A printed star atlas. Its signature is the Sky plate, where every check is a dot sized by tremor strength and plotted by hours since dose and date. The returns-after line is drawn like a meridian, and an inspector shows the selected check.
